# SIH Problem Analysis & Literature Review

## 1. Problem Definition & Context
In rural India, micro-entrepreneurs (kirana store owners, artisans, processing units, poultry/dairy farmers) face acute barriers when launching or scaling enterprises:
- **"Copycat Enterprise" Trap:** Herding behavior without analyzing local demand saturation.
- **The Hyper-Local Data Void:** Absence of formal digital footprints, reviews, or commercial registries at the village/gram panchayat level.
- **Financial Structuring & Subsidy Blindness:** Inability to separate CapEx from OpEx, account for seasonal cash-flow variations, or navigate complex government credit-linked subsidies (PMMY MUDRA, PMEGP, PM SVANidhi, NRLM SHG linkages).
- **Language & Literacy Barriers:** Existing digital portals (Jan Samarth, MyScheme) are form-heavy, English/Hindi dominant, and text-first.

## 2. Competitive Landscape & Literature Review

### A. National Landscape (India)
1. **Haqdarshak:**
   - *Model:* Tech-enabled field agent (Haqdarshak cadre) model linking citizens to welfare schemes.
   - *Strength:* Massive scheme database and grassroots trust.
   - *Gap:* Purely entitlement/scheme focused; does not conduct hyper-local business viability or competitor saturation assessments before recommending a business activity.

2. **Kaleidofin (`ki score`):**
   - *Model:* Inclusive fintech providing Lending-as-a-Service (LaaS) and alternative credit scoring for informal sector workers.
   - *Strength:* ML-driven risk profiling for thin-file borrowers.
   - *Gap:* Underwriting platform built for institutional lenders (banks, NBFCs, MFIs), not a direct pre-venture advisory or financial structuring tool for the entrepreneur.

3. **Aye Finance / Paisalo Digital / Satya MicroCapital:**
   - *Model:* Cluster-based MSME/micro-enterprise NBFCs.
   - *Strength:* High-touch field assessment for underserved micro-enterprises.
   - *Gap:* For-profit lenders focused on origination. They lack impartial business viability advisory, margin capital optimization, or government subsidy navigation.

4. **Khatabook / OkCredit / Vyapar:**
   - *Model:* Digital ledger and micro-accounting applications.
   - *Strength:* Extensive rural/semi-urban merchant adoption.
   - *Gap:* Post-facto record keeping only; zero pre-launch feasibility analysis or loan structuring.

5. **Jan Samarth / MyScheme (Government Portals):**
   - *Model:* Centralized portals for credit-linked government subsidy schemes.
   - *Strength:* Official rules, direct integration with sanctioning banks.
   - *Gap:* Complex form-filling, static eligibility checks, zero market demand insights, and zero guidance on cash-flow stress testing.

### B. Global Benchmarks
1. **Boost Capital (Cambodia & Philippines):**
   - *Model:* AI-driven conversational loan onboarding and financial literacy via WhatsApp, Telegram, and Facebook Messenger.
   - *Takeaway for SIH:* Proves that micro-entrepreneurs adopt voice/chat interfaces 5x faster than standalone native applications.

2. **Apollo Agriculture (Kenya & East Africa):**
   - *Model:* Bundled financing, agronomic advisory, and high-quality inputs using satellite data and machine learning.
   - *Takeaway for SIH:* Combines macro data (satellite indices) with micro merchant inputs to assess true earning potential.

## 3. The Winning Differentiator (Our Strategic Moat)
Our solution bridges the gap between **business feasibility** and **formal financial structuring**:
- **Voice-First & Multilingual ("Bharat Mode"):** Operates conversationally in regional languages via Bhashini.
- **Hybrid Local Feasibility Engine:** Blends macro open data (Census, road access, APMC mandi rates) with conversational micro ground-truth.
- **Deterministic Financial Engine ("Calculation Passport"):** Never hallucinating loan figures; computing accurate CapEx/OpEx, seasonal DSCR, and optimal government scheme subsidies.
- **Bank-Ready Bilingual 1-Page DPR:** Downloadable PDF with verifiable QR code designed for rural bank branch managers.
