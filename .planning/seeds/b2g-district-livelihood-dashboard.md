# Seed: B2G District Livelihood Dashboard & Account Aggregator

## Vision
Transform the individual advisory assistant into an institutional command center for district administrations, State Rural Livelihood Missions (SRLM / NRLM), and District Industries Centres (DIC).

## Trigger Condition
Activate this workstream once the core Advisory Engine (Module 1) and DPR Generator (Module 2) are completed and validated with mock user evaluations.

## Scope Ideas
1. **District Failure Risk & Saturation Heatmap:**
   - Visualize geographic clusters where specific micro-businesses are becoming over-saturated (preventing bad loan disbursements).
2. **Sanction & Disbursement Tracking:**
   - Track DPRs generated vs. loans sanctioned across Lead District Banks (LDB) and Regional Rural Banks (RRBs).
3. **Account Aggregator (AA) Integration (Sahamati / Setu):**
   - Seamless consent-based pulling of applicant bank statements to automatically verify margin capital and past transaction regularity.
