## 2. Logic Chain
1. **The Distribution Problem:** Direct-to-consumer rural digital marketing suffers from severe CAC inflation (₹4,200–₹6,500/loan) and fatal conversion rates (3–5%) due to the rural digital-financial literacy gap and high-stakes trust deficit.
2. **The Phygital Solution:** Mobilizing existing rural institutional and commercial intermediaries—CSCs, Bank Sakhis, and DRPs—provides instant physical trust, village presence, and hardware capabilities (PCs, scanners, biometrics).
3. **The Economic Alignment:** By offering a tiered incentive structure (₹250–₹350 per completed DPR, plus 0.5% or ₹1,500 loan sanction bonus), our platform increases intermediary monthly earnings by 30% to 150%, turning routine cash agents into loyal asset-sourcing specialists.
4. **Institutional De-risking:** Anchoring the appraisal process with NABARD DDMs, LDMs, and KVK technical clearance certificates reduces bank loan rejections from ~75% down to under 20%.
5. **Full-Stack Commercialization:** Transitioning newly funded agro-processors onto ONDC via Mystore/eSamudaay ensures guaranteed market linkage, eliminating exploitative mandi discounts and securing loan repayment stability.
