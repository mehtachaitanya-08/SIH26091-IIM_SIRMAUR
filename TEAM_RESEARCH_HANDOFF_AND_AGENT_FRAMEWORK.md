## 1. Executive Context & Core Mission (For Team Members)

### 1.1 The Strategic Problem We Are Solving
In rural India, millions of aspiring entrepreneurs and existing micro-enterprises operate entirely in the informal economy. They face two catastrophic barriers:
1. **The Incubation & Formalization Trap (Mode A):** High-subsidy government schemes (PMEGP 35% grant, PMFME 35% grant, PM Vishwakarma 5% credit, NLM 50% subsidy) exist, but applying requires a Detailed Project Report (DPR), Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) calculations, and complex regulatory clearances. Local middlemen (*dalals*) exploit rural entrepreneurs, charging 10%–25% cuts simply to format paperwork.
2. **The Predatory Debt Trap (Mode B):** Over 68% of existing rural micro-entrepreneurs carry informal debt from village moneylenders (*Sahukars / Mahajans*) charging **3% to 5% per month (36% to 60% APR)**. Families spend 40%–70% of their net surplus servicing interest alone without ever amortizing the principal.

### 1.2 Our Platform Solution
We are building a dual-mode platform powered by a **Zero-Hallucination Deterministic Financial Engine**:
- **Mode A (Incubation):** Evaluates local business feasibility, calculates parametric CapEx/OpEx, optimizes government subsidies, runs 12-month agricultural seasonality stress-tests, and generates an official 1-Page Bilingual Bank-Ready DPR PDF.
- **Mode B (Debt Restructuring):** Audits unorganized debt, verifies statutory protections (*Rule of Damdupat* and State Moneylenders Acts), and algorithmically consolidates informal loans into 4%–9.5% formal institutional credit (MUDRA, KCC, Vishwakarma), freeing up 35%–55% of household disposable income.

### 1.3 Why We Need Distributed Agent Research
To make our advisory engine hyper-realistic across India's 324+ Lakh rural micro-enterprises, we need granular, verified financial and regulatory dossiers across **24 distinct business domains**. Because our team is working across multiple devices and machines, we use this **Standardized Agent Research Framework** so that every team member's research output is 100% structured, uniform, and database-ready.

## 5. Master Prompt Template to Deploy on Any Device

When initiating research on Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or a local agent on any device, copy-paste the exact prompt below, replacing only the variables in brackets `[ ]`.

```markdown
# TASK: Authoritative Rural MSME Domain Research Dossier
**Domain Code:** [e.g., D08]  
**Domain Name:** [e.g., Merchant Edible Oil Expellers & Cold-Press Ghanis]  
**Parent User Basket:** [e.g., B3: Food Milling, Processing & Local Packaging]  
**Target Context:** Smart India Hackathon 2026 - Rural MSME Financial Advisory & Restructuring Platform  
**Geographic Target:** Rural & Semi-Urban India (Focus: UP, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan, Maharashtra)  

---

### ROLE & INSTRUCTIONS:
You are an expert team comprising a Rural MSME Industrial Researcher, an Indian Micro-Enterprise Banking Officer, and a Regulatory Compliance Auditor.

Generate a comprehensive, publication-grade research dossier for this domain. You must not invent or hallucinate data. Ground your work in official Government of India sources (Ministry of MSME, MoFPI, DAHD, KVIC, RBI, CPCB, NSSO, ASUSE).

Your output must follow the strict 8-Section Research Contract without omitting any section:

### SECTION 1: MACRO DEMOGRAPHICS & MARKET SIZING
- Official classification: NIC 2008 5-digit code, statutory sector (Manufacturing, Service, Trade).
- National volume and rural enterprise count based on MoSPI / NSSO / ASUSE data.
- Catchment demand: Village population or farming radius required to sustain one viable unit.
- Key consumption drivers and urban vs. rural demand differentiation.

### SECTION 2: STANDARDIZED CAPEX BILL OF MATERIALS
Provide an itemized table with:
- Line Item (Equipment/Machinery/Civil Shed/Electricals).
- Technical Specifications (Power rating in HP, Phase 1 or 3, capacity in kg/hr or units/day).
- Reputed Regional Manufacturing Hubs & OEMs (e.g., Rajkot, Ludhiana, Coimbatore, Ghaziabad).
- Realistic Benchmark Cost in INR (Base model vs. Commercial model).
- Pre-operative expenses (licensing, site preparation, connection deposits).
- Total Setup CapEx Range.

### SECTION 3: RECURRING OPEX & WORKING CAPITAL CYCLE
- Monthly Operating Expenses Table (Raw materials, 3-phase electricity tariff, skilled/unskilled labor, rent, consumables, packaging, maintenance).
- Operating Cycle & Working Capital Duration:
  - Raw material inventory holding days.
  - Work-in-progress / curing / processing days.
  - Finished goods inventory days.
  - Customer credit (*udhaar / bahi-khata*) receivables days.
  - Net Working Capital Requirement in INR.

### SECTION 4: DETERMINISTIC UNIT ECONOMICS MODEL
- Core unit throughput (e.g., per kg, per quintal, per hour, per liter).
- Input procurement cost vs. output realization price (spread analysis).
- Byproduct valorization (e.g., oil cake, bran, dung slurry) with exact pricing.
- Blended Gross Margin (%) and Net Operating Surplus (₹/month).
- Break-Even Analysis: Minimum capacity utilization (%) needed to service operating costs and bank debt.

### SECTION 5: 12-MONTH AGRICULTURAL SEASONALITY MULTIPLIER ARRAY
Provide an array of 12 numbers representing monthly revenue multipliers relative to the annual base (1.0 = normal month):
`[M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6, M7, M8, M9, M10, M11, M12]` (Starting M1 = April, financial year start).
- Explain the agricultural reasons for peak months (harvest arrivals, festival demand, wedding season).
- Explain lean months (monsoon shutdowns, field flooding, summer dry period).
- State the minimum lean-month cash flow stress factor.

### SECTION 6: REGULATORY & COMPLIANCE BLUEPRINT
Itemized compliance matrix covering:
- Food Safety / Industry License: FSSAI (Registration vs State License thresholds based on daily throughput capacity, not just turnover), Agmark, BIS.
- Environmental Clearance: CPCB / SPCB categorization (White, Green, Orange) with specific notification reference. State if CTE/CTO is exempt.
- Electricity Mandate: Commercial connected load (HP / kW), 3-phase LT requirement.
- Local Body Approvals: Gram Panchayat Trade NOC, Shops & Establishment.
- Tax & Enterprise Identity: Udyam Registration, GST exemption threshold (₹40L for goods, ₹20L for services).

### SECTION 7: GOVERNMENT SCHEME & SUBSIDY DECISION RULES
- Primary Central Government Scheme fit:
  - If Manufacturing/Processing: Evaluate PMEGP (35% rural special subsidy, 5% equity) vs. PMFME (35% subsidy up to ₹10L).
  - If Livestock: Evaluate National Livestock Mission (50% capital subsidy) + KCC (net 4% interest).
  - If Artisanal Trades: Evaluate PM Vishwakarma (₹15k toolkit + Tranche 1 ₹1L @ 5% + Tranche 2 ₹2L @ 5%).
  - If Retail/Service: Evaluate PMMY MUDRA (Shishu/Kishor/Tarun) and PM SVANidhi.
  - If Post-Harvest Storage/Bio-Waste: Evaluate Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF 3% interest subvention up to ₹2 Cr).
- State exact promoter margin money requirement (5% to 15%).
- State typical loan amortization tenure and interest rate range.

### SECTION 8: OPERATIONAL RISK REGISTER & ALGORITHMIC MITIGATION
Create a table with:
- Risk Factor (e.g., commodity price spikes, livestock mortality, power cuts, credit defaults).
- Severity (High, Medium, Critical).
- Algorithmic Mitigation Rule (Exact math or advisory logic our AI engine should trigger).

OUTPUT FORMAT: Clean, rigorous GitHub-flavored Markdown. No placeholder text, no generic filler.
```

## 6. Pre-Submission Quality Audit Checklist

Before committing any completed dossier to the repository, the team member must verify it against these 8 Quality Gates:

- [ ] **Gate 1: Daily Capacity Threshold Verified for FSSAI?**  
  *Did you check if daily processing exceeds 100 kg/day (flour) or 100 L/day (oil)? If so, it requires an FSSAI State License, not just basic registration.*
- [ ] **Gate 2: CPCB Pollution Category Grounded?**  
  *Did you cite the official CPCB White Category or Green Category classification with specific criteria?*
- [ ] **Gate 3: PMEGP Trading Prohibition Respected?**  
  *Did you ensure retail trading is NOT matched with PMEGP capital subsidies (unless Khadi/VI specific)?*
- [ ] **Gate 4: Seasonality Multiplier Array Validated?**  
  *Are there exactly 12 monthly numbers in Section 5 with an average of approximately 1.0?*
- [ ] **Gate 5: Working Capital Tied to Operating Cycle?**  
  *Is the working capital buffer derived from inventory days + credit days, rather than an arbitrary flat number?*
- [ ] **Gate 6: Biological Reality Checked (For Livestock)?**  
  *Did you account for the Year-1 zero-meat revenue lag for goats or the dry period for dairy cattle?*
- [ ] **Gate 7: Machine Specifications & Sourcing Hubs Named?**  
  *Are real machine specs (HP, phase, capacity) and authentic regional hubs (Rajkot, Ludhiana, etc.) listed?*
- [ ] **Gate 8: No LLM Arithmetic in Output?**  
  *Are the CapEx and OpEx numbers cross-checked with basic addition so there are no arithmetic inconsistencies?*
