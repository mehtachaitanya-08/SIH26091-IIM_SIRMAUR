1. What we are building (from the official statement)
1.1 The official brief, in short
The money rule: the beneficiary puts in 10% of project cost; the loan covers 90%, through State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs, the state bodies that pass central loans to beneficiaries).
The two loan tiers:
Tier
Project cost
Loan
Interest
Repayment
Micro Finance
up to ₹1.40 lakh
up to 90%, max ₹1.25 lakh
6.5% a year
3 years incl. 3-month moratorium (a starting period with no repayment)
Term Loan
₹1.40 lakh – ₹50.00 lakh
up to 90%, max ₹45 lakh
8% a year
7 years incl. 6-month moratorium (12 months for plantation and construction, per the MoSJE page)
The calculator must apply both the 90% rule and the cap. Example: a ₹1.40 lakh project gets a loan of ₹1.25 lakh, not ₹1.26 lakh.
Inputs: location (village/block/district), available margin capital, and business category.
Who it is for: "rural and semi-urban geographies".
Module 1, Hyper-local feasibility report:
Market reach within 5–10 km, and distribution channels.
Unserved or underserved niches.
SWOT.
Threats: supply bottlenecks, seasonal demand, dependence on a single buyer.
Competitor density in the block.
Pricing strategy and local market value, based on regional purchasing power.
Module 2, Smart financial calculator and scheme router:
Project cost = margin ÷ 10%.
Loan = 90%, within the cap.
Choose the tier.
Produce the quarterly EMI and moratorium schedule, operating costs and working capital.
The app must be NLP-powered and multilingual.
1.2 Who the users are
The tiers above match NSFDC (National Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation, under MoSJE).
Its page says an applicant "must belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) community and possess a valid caste certificate".
Family income must not exceed ₹5.00 lakh a year, rural or urban, effective 7 January 2026.
Source: https://devmosje.negd.in/?p=15629 (opened 7 Nov 2026). This is a MoSJE development-site copy; confirm it on nsfdc.nic.in.
So the dossiers are written for SC entrepreneurs at the ₹1.40 lakh and up-to-₹50 lakh project sizes.
1.3 Our solution
Core = Module 1 + Module 2 exactly as above. Judges will score against these.
Our edge comes from two things:
The dossiers (§4) give both modules real, sourced numbers for each business type.
A compliance layer: for each rule, an official link, the exact quote and a last-verified date (the pattern from our compliance matrix).
1.4 Optional add-on: existing-loans check (was "Mode B")
This is beyond the brief. It comes second and is labelled as an extra.
What it does: if a user already has informal loans, it shows what those cost against the scheme's 6.5% or 8%.
Moneylender interest caps are State law. One example is the "Damdupat" rule, under which total interest may not exceed the loan amount. Rajasthan's Money-Lenders Act s.27 has it. Check the user's own State's Act before saying anything.
RBI (Reserve Bank of India): under its Digital Lending Directions 2025, an app becomes a Lending Service Provider only when it works as an agent of a bank or NBFC (a non-bank lender licensed by RBI), doing lending work for it, e.g. customer acquisition. Our default is information only, with no lender partnership.
SEBI (the market regulator): its definition of investment advice "shall include financial planning". Get the lawyer's view before adding any personal advice feature.
Source: https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/aug-2026/1785912187595.pdf
Numbers: no headline statistics without an official source (§7.3).

8. Glossary (plain words)
Term
Meaning
NSFDC / SCA
National Scheduled Castes Finance and Development Corporation; it lends through State Channelizing Agencies (state bodies that pass the loan to the beneficiary)
Moratorium
Months at the start of a loan when no repayment is due
Working-capital cycle
Days money is stuck in stock and customer credit before it comes back as cash
NIC code
Government code for a type of business activity (NIC 2008 old, NIC 2025 new)
CPCB / SPCB; White / Green / Orange / Red
Central and State pollution boards; categories from least to most polluting, deciding which permissions are needed
ASUSE / AIDIS / NAFIS
Official surveys: small non-farm businesses; household debt; rural financial inclusion
Damdupat
Old rule, in some State laws, that total interest can't exceed the loan amount
Negative list
Activities a scheme refuses to fund
GeM
Government e-Marketplace, the official purchase portal with listed prices
LSP
Lending Service Provider: an agent doing lending work for a bank or NBFC, regulated through that lender
Deep link
The exact page or PDF address, not a site's home page
