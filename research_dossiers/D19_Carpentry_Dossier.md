## 6. Compliance

### 6.1 Pollution Categorization (CPCB 2025/2026)
* **Carpentry & wooden furniture without spray painting (motorized, no saw mill):** **WHITE (Pollution Index 0.0)** under Sector 60.2 [S7] p.15. Exemption with written intimation to SPCB [S9][S10].
* **Carpentry with spray painting:** **GREEN (Index 25.0)** under Sector 60.1 [S7] p.15.
* **Saw mills (sawing logs into planks):** **GREEN (Index 30.0)** under Sector 164.0 [S7] p.45.
* **Steam-heated Wood Seasoning Chamber:** **GREEN (Index 50.0)** under Sector 184.0 [S7] p.49.
* **Modular Furniture with boiler:** **ORANGE (Index 60.0)** [S7] p.14.
* **Maharashtra White List:** MPCB Circular 16.06.2025 covers "Carpentry without seasoning without electrical motor" (item 32) and "Wooden furniture basic items" (item 219) [S8].
* **Timber Regulations:** Buying raw timber logs requires State Forest Department transit passes and saw-machine registration.

### 6.2 GST
* Exclusive supply of manufactured furniture qualifies for **₹40 Lakh** threshold [S12]. If providing on-site repair/fitting services, standard **₹20 Lakh** threshold applies.
