About Industry

A Pottery Products Unit is a small to medium scale enterprise that produces a variety of handcrafted and machine made pottery items using natural clay. The process involves clay preparation, molding/shaping (using potter’s wheel or molds), drying, firing in kilns, glazing or painting, and finishing. Products include flower pots, kitchenware, dinner sets, decorative vases, figurines, and art pieces that are both functional and aesthetic. Pottery is valued for its eco friendliness, durability, and cultural appeal, making it popular among urban households, gift markets, interior decorators, and export buyers. The unit requires basic machinery and tools such as potter’s wheels, kilns, molding equipment, and hand tools, along with skilled artisans for intricate designs. With growing demand for eco friendly home décor and handcrafted products, a Pottery Products Unit offers excellent business potential. The venture is scalable, profitable, and promotes traditional craftsmanship, while also allowing expansion into customized and thematic pottery products for retail, exhibitions, and online marketplaces.

Pottery Products
Pottery products are items crafted from clay, shaped, dried, and fired at high temperatures to create durable and decorative objects. Common examples include bowls, plates, vases, and tiles. They can be categorized into earthenware, stoneware, and porcelain, each differing in durability and finish, and often hold cultural significance.
