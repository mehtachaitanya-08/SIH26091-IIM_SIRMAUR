* **Covered Trade:** Explicitly covered under **Trade 17: Tailor (Darzi)** [S7] p.16.
* **Benefits:**
  * Free basic skill training + **₹500/day stipend** [S8].
  * Modern toolkit e-voucher of **₹15,000** [S7].
  * Collateral-free concessional loan: **₹1,00,000 (Tranche 1, 18 mos) + ₹2,00,000 (Tranche 2, 30 mos) @ 5.0% interest** [S7].
  * Digital incentive: ₹1 per digital transaction (up to ₹100/mo) [S8].
* **Critical Exclusion Rule:** Blocked if any PMEGP loan sanctioned in past 5 years. Prior MUDRA/SVANidhi must be fully repaid [S7] p.27.

### 7.2 NSFDC & PMEGP
* **NSFDC:** Micro Finance (≤₹1.40L @ 6.5%) and Term Loan @ 8.0% [S1]. 40% reserved for women [S1].
* **PMEGP:** 35% rural subsidy, but cannot combine with NSFDC SCA loan [S9].
