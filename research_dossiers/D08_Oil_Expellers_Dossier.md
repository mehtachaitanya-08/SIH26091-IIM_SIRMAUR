**Domain Code:** D08  
**Short Name:** Oil_Expellers  
**Parent User Basket:** B3 Food Milling, Processing & Local Packaging (अनाज पिसाई, तेल एवं खाद्य उद्योग)  
**Target Beneficiaries:** Scheduled Caste (SC) rural entrepreneurs (annual family income up to ₹5.00 Lakh) borrowing through NSFDC State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs).  
**Geographic Target:** Rural & Semi-Urban India (Focus States: Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Maharashtra).  
**Date of Verification:** 08 November 2026

## 1. CLASSIFICATION & MARKET

### 1.1 Official Classification & Codes
- **NIC 2008 Codes:** 
  - `10402`: Manufacture of edible vegetable and animal oils and fats (cold pressed, unrefined, expeller extracted) [S1].
  - *(Note: Code 10401 represents hydrogenated oil/vanaspati and is not applicable to micro expellers) [S1].*
- **NIC 2025 Codes:** 
  - `104002`: Cold-pressed / Kachhi ghani vegetable oil extraction, mustard oil expelling, and unrefined edible oil packaging [S2].
- **Statutory Sector:** Manufacturing / Agro-Processing (Micro-Enterprise).

### 1.2 Enterprise Population & National Sizing
- **ASUSE Benchmark:** According to MoSPI Annual Survey of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises (ASUSE 2023-24), rural unincorporated manufacturing enterprises stand at 114.38 Lakh units [S3]. Rural mechanical oil expellers, wooden cold-press ghanis, and village oil crushing units account for approximately 3.15 Lakh operational units [S3].

### 1.3 Catchment Demand & Local Feasibility
- **Feasibility Radius:** 6–10 km radial catchment covering 8 to 12 agricultural revenue villages.
- **Crop & Household Base Required:** A viable 6-bolt expeller unit requires an annual oilseed command area of 400 to 600 hectares (predominantly mustard, groundnut, or sesame) or a consumer catchment of 1,200 to 1,800 rural households consuming 2.5 to 3.5 Litres of cooking oil per month [S4].
- **Demand Drivers:** 
  - Strong rural and semi-urban preference for pungency (*jhanjh*) and unadulterated cold-pressed mustard oil (*Kachhi Ghani*) over chemically refined oils.
  - Huge localized demand for freshly expelled oil cake (*sarson ki khal*) among village dairy farmers for bovine protein nutrition.
  - Farmers seeking custom expelling (toll crushing) where they supply raw home-grown mustard and pay ₹6 to ₹8 per kg of seed pressed, retaining both oil and cake.
- **Rural vs. Semi-Urban Dynamics:**
  - *Rural Market Unit:* Operates heavily on custom toll crushing during harvest months and retails unbranded loose oil in consumer containers.
  - *Semi-Urban / Tehsil Center:* Focuses on merchant processing—buying graded seed in mandis, filtering, bottling in 1-Litre food-grade PET bottles, and supplying local retail kiranas.

### 1.4 Unserved and Underserved Niches
- **Single-Origin Cold-Press Wooden Ghani Oil (*Kolhu*):** Premium health-conscious rural and peri-urban consumers are willing to pay a 40%–60% premium (₹220–₹260/Litre) for cold-pressed oil extracted below 45°C without frictional heat degradation.
- **De-stoned & Filtered Packaged Mustard Oil:** Traditional expellers produce cloudy oil with sediment; introducing a vibratory seed destoner and plate-and-frame filter press produces crystal-clear golden oil that commands a ₹15/Litre retail premium.
- **High-Protein Clean Cattle Cake Pellets:** Grinding and packing expeller cake into clean, moisture-proof 50 kg bags for dairy cooperatives avoids mold and dust contamination.
- **How an SC Entrepreneur Spots Niches in the Block:**
  1. Inspect the District Mandi arrivals data on Agmarknet for mustard / rapeseed between February and April.
  2. Test local loose oil samples from 5 village grocers for adulteration or low pungency (indicative of solvent mixing).
  3. Interview 20 dairy farmers at the local milk collection center regarding what they pay for packaged mustard cake versus local loose cake.

### 3.2 Working Capital Cycle (In Days)
- **Raw Oilseed Holding Days:** 15 Days (Buffer stock of graded mustard seed).
- **Processing & Filtration Days:** 2 Days (Crushing, sediment settling, filter pressing).
- **Finished Oil & Cake Inventory Days:** 8 Days (Bottled oil and bagged cake awaiting pickup).
- **Customer Credit (*Udhaar*) / Kirana Receivables:** 15 Days (Credit extended to retail shops and dairy farmers).
- **Total Working Capital Cycle Length:** `15 + 2 + 8 + 15 = 40 Days` [S8].
- **Operating Working Capital Formula:**
  $$\text{Working Capital Requirement} = \left(\frac{\text{Cycle Days}}{30}\right) \times \text{Monthly Operational Cost} + \text{Rotating Seed Stock}$$
  $$\text{Working Capital} = \left(\frac{40}{30}\right) \times ₹49,250 + ₹2,64,333 = ₹65,667 + ₹2,64,333 = ₹3,30,000 \text{ [S8]}$$

## 4. UNIT ECONOMICS & PRICING

### 4.1 Input-Output Mass Balance & Spread Analysis
- **Core Processing Material:** Rapeseed & Mustard Seed (*Brassica juncea*).
- **Official Mandi Procurement Price (MSP RMS 2025-26):** ₹5,950 per quintal (₹59.50/kg) [S10].
- **Physical Extraction Yields per 100 kg Raw Mustard Seed:**
  - Pure Filtered Mustard Oil: **34.0 kg** (approx. 37.3 Litres @ 0.91 kg/L specific gravity) [S8].
  - Mustard Oil Cake (*Sarson Khal*): **64.0 kg** (high protein bovine feed) [S8].
  - Evaporative Moisture / Cleaning Wastage: **2.0 kg** [S8].
- **Output Realization per 100 kg Seed:**
  - Mustard Oil Sales: 34 kg @ ₹155.00/kg (equivalent to ₹141.00/Litre bulk) = **₹5,270.00** [S8].
  - Mustard Cake Sales: 64 kg @ ₹28.50/kg (local dairy farmer pickup) = **₹1,824.00** [S8].
  - **Total Realization per Quintal:** `₹5,270.00 + ₹1,824.00 = ₹7,094.00` [S8].
  - **Gross Value Addition Spread:** `₹7,094.00 - ₹5,950.00 = ₹1,144.00 per quintal` (**19.23% Gross Spread**) [S8].

### 4.2 Monthly Revenue & Profitability Model (Normalized Operating Month)
- **Monthly Processing Volume:** 10,000 kg (10 MT / 100 quintals) of mustard seed [S8].
- **Total Raw Material Cost:** 100 quintals @ ₹5,950 = ₹5,95,000.
- **Total Gross Output Realization:** 100 quintals @ ₹7,094 = ₹7,09,400.
- **Total Gross Operating Spread:** `₹7,09,400 - ₹5,95,000 = ₹1,14,400` [S8].
- **Additional Custom Toll Crushing (2 MT @ ₹7/kg):** `2,000 kg @ ₹7 = ₹14,000`.
- **Total Blended Gross Margin:** `₹1,14,400 + ₹14,000 = ₹1,28,400` [S8].
- **Net Operating Surplus:** 
  $$\text{Net Monthly Surplus} = \text{Gross Margin (₹1,28,400)} - \text{Operating Overheads (₹49,250)} = ₹79,150 \text{ [S8]}$$
  *(In post-harvest peak season, processing 20 MT doubles net surplus to ₹1,45,000/month).*

### 4.3 Break-Even Capacity Utilization
- **Monthly Fixed Overheads:** ₹38,000 (Rent, wages, electricity base, loan interest).
- **Gross Spread per Quintal:** ₹1,144.00 (₹11.44 per kg seed).
- **Break-Even Monthly Seed Crushing Volume:** 
  $$\text{Break-Even Volume} = \frac{₹38,000}{₹11.44} = 3,322 \text{ kg per month (approx. 3.32 MT) [S8]}$$
- **Break-Even Capacity Utilization:** Approximately **22.1%** of single-shift expelling capacity.

## 7. SCHEME FIT & SUBSIDY DECISION RULES

### 7.1 Primary Scheme: NSFDC (National Scheduled Castes Finance & Development Corporation)
- **Target Category:** Scheduled Caste (SC) entrepreneurs possessing valid community certificate [S9].
- **Family Income Ceiling:** Annual family income must not exceed **₹5.00 Lakh** (effective 07 January 2026) [S9].
- **Applicable Tiers for D08:**
  1. *Micro Finance Scheme (MFS):* Project cost up to ₹1.40 Lakh. Promoter equity: 10% (₹14,000); NSFDC covers 90% up to maximum cap of ₹1,25,000 at **6.5% interest rate p.a.** Amortization tenure: 3 years including 3-month moratorium [S9]. Ideal for cold-press tabletop wooden rotary ghani.
  2. *Term Loan Scheme:* Project cost ₹1.40 Lakh to ₹50.00 Lakh. Promoter equity: 10% (₹85,000 on ₹8.5L unit); NSFDC covers 90% (₹7,65,000 on ₹8.5L unit) at **8.0% interest rate p.a.** Amortization tenure: 7 years including 6-month moratorium [S9]. Ideal for commercial 6-bolt expeller and filter unit.
- **Delivery Mechanism:** Direct disbursement through State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs).

### 7.2 Secondary Scheme Compatibility & Subsidy Decision Rules
- **PMFME (Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises):** 
  - *Fit Status:* **PREMIER SCHEME FIT / HIGH-SUBSIDY MATCH** [S21].
  - *Subsidy:* **35% credit-linked capital subsidy** on eligible machinery and shed cost, capped at **₹10.00 Lakh** [S21].
  - *Own Contribution:* 10% promoter equity.
  - *Applicability:* Mustard seed oil expelling is the designated One District One Product (ODOP) across multiple districts in Rajasthan (Alwar, Bharatpur), UP (Mathura, Agra, Aligarh), and MP (Morena, Bhind) [S21].
- **PMEGP (Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme):** 
  - *Fit Status:* **ELIGIBLE / ALTERNATIVE MATCH** [S22].
  - *Category:* Agro-based Food Processing Manufacturing.
  - *Subsidy:* **35% margin money subsidy** for rural SC entrepreneurs with 5% promoter equity [S22].
- **Combinability Rule:** Capital subsidy double-dipping is prohibited. An applicant cannot combine PMFME grant with PMEGP grant. However, NSFDC Term Loan at 8% interest can be utilized as the primary institutional term loan to unlock PMFME capital subsidy subject to SCA and bank approval [S9, S21].

## 8. RISKS, SWOT & COMPETITOR DENSITY

### 8.1 Operational Risk Register & Algorithmic Mitigation Rules

| Risk Factor | Severity | Early Warning Indicator | App Algorithmic Decision Rule (If-Then) |
|:---|:---:|:---|:---|
| **Raw Mustard Seed Mandi Price Spikes** | Critical | Mandi wholesale mustard price surges >15% without corresponding retail oil price rise | `IF mustard_mandi_price > 1.15 * base_price THEN adjust expelling focus to custom toll crushing (charging cash service fee) to insulate from commodity inventory risk.` |
| **Expeller Worm Wear & Residual Oil Loss** | High | Oil content in expelled mustard cake exceeds 8.5% (tested by touch/lab) | `IF cake_oil_content > 8.0% THEN trigger maintenance notification: schedule hard-facing welding of expeller worm screws to restore 34% oil recovery efficiency.` |
| **Filter Press Leakage & Oil Turbidity** | Medium | Filtered oil appears cloudy or sediment visible in settling inspection glass | `IF filtered_oil_turbidity == TRUE THEN halt packaging line AND prompt operator: replace clogged canvas filter cloth pads with fresh unbleached cotton filter sheets.` |
| **Customer Credit Risk from Village Grocers** | High | Kirana store outstanding payment aging >30 days; >25% of working capital trapped | `IF retail_credit_aging > 30_days THEN restrict bottled oil dispatches to cash-on-delivery (COD) AND offer 1.5% prompt payment discount.` |

### 8.2 SWOT Analysis

#### Micro Finance Tier (₹1.40 Lakh Setup)
- **Strengths:** Low capital threshold; minimal interest obligation (₹1,25,000 @ 6.5%); produces high-value authentic cold-press unheated oil.
- **Weaknesses:** Low processing capacity (15 kg/hr); cannot compete on volume with commercial mandi mills.
- **Opportunities:** Branding artisanal cold-pressed "Kachhi Ghani" mustard/groundnut oil for health-conscious local consumers.
- **Threats:** Local power fluctuations disrupting single-phase motor operation.

#### Term Loan Tier (₹8.50 Lakh Setup)
- **Strengths:** High daily throughput (500–700 kg seed/day); automated seed cleaning and filtration; dual revenue from oil and cattle cake.
- **Weaknesses:** Working capital intensive; exposed to raw mustard price fluctuations.
- **Opportunities:** Supplying packaged 1-Litre bottles to block-level supermarkets, rural retail chains, and dairy cooperative members.
- **Threats:** Unfair competition from illegal blending of cheap palm olein in unbranded loose mustard oil sold in haats.

### 8.3 Competitor Density & Market Estimation Methodology
- **Data Source for Density Mapping:** District Agriculture Statistics & Mandi Board Oilseed Production Data [S4, S10].
- **Estimation Metric:** 
  $$\text{Target Expeller Density} = \frac{\text{Block Mustard Production (MT)}}{250 \text{ MT annual seed crushing per viable 6-bolt unit}}$$
- **Rule of Thumb:** If more than 2 commercial expellers operate in a single panchayat cluster, avoid standard mechanical expelling. Instead, establish a **Wood-Cold-Press Rotary Ghani (Marachekku / Lakdi Ghani)** to capture the premium unrefined oil segment.
