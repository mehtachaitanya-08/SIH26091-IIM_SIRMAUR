Our design choice: the [S5] unit's fixed assets of ₹1.00 lakh, plus a smaller working capital, kept under the ₹1.40 lakh ceiling.

| 3 | Item-by-item prices (potter's wheel, kiln) | VENDOR QUOTE NEEDED | — |

**Scaling the micro unit down so the working capital fits** (cycle-days rule, framework G11):
- Net cycle of the [S5] unit: 64.8 days (§3).
- Monthly cash cost that ₹40,000 can carry: ESTIMATE 0.40 ÷ (64.8 ÷ 30) = ₹0.185 lakh a month, against [S5]'s ₹0.71 lakh.
- So the micro unit can run at about ESTIMATE 0.185 ÷ 0.71 = 26% of [S5]'s output. That means sales of about ESTIMATE 9.24 × 0.26 = **₹2.40 lakh a year**, not [S5]'s ₹9.24 lakh.
- **App rule (our design choice):** never pair the micro size with [S5]'s full sales.

**Loan at the ceiling:**
- Project ₹1,40,000 [S1] → "up to 90%" [S1] = at most ₹1,26,000 → capped at ₹1,25,000 [S1].
- Own share is **at least** ₹15,000 [S1].

PM Vishwakarma's toolkit e-voucher of "₹15,000" [S14] p.4 could cover part of the tools for an eligible potter (see §7).
